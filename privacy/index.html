Privacy Policy

Updated 12 September 2026

Finewild is a free discovery app for fashion, beauty and home design. In the Communite catalog update, browsing, favorites and the bag work without an account. The app has no subscriptions, paid placement, or advertising. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

What we collect

  • Account. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your name and email address (with Sign in with Apple you can hide your real email). This is stored with our database provider, Supabase, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, eu-central-1).
  • Favorites. Favorites in the Communite catalog are stored on your device and are not uploaded for account synchronization. Earlier versions stored account-linked favorites with Supabase. Those historical records remain separate from the new catalog and are not reassigned to different products.
  • Catalog delivery. Public product information and images are hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare receives connection information, including your IP address, to deliver that content. Catalog requests do not include your Finewild account details, bag, addresses or saved-favorite identifiers.
  • Usage analytics. We use PostHog's EU service to understand app use and improve reliability. It receives app lifecycle events and review-prompt events, app and device information (such as app version, device model, operating system, and language), and a randomly assigned identifier. When you sign in, analytics can be linked to your account identifier. PostHog can derive approximate location from your IP address; Finewild does not request your device's GPS location. This data is never sold or shared with brands in a form that identifies you.
  • Data kept on your device. Your bag and saved delivery addresses are stored locally. Finewild does not upload those addresses or collect card or payment information. You enter delivery and payment details on Communite's website when you choose to order.

What we never do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers — the app contains no third-party ad SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of personal data, to anyone, ever.
  • No payment processing — orders are completed on Communite's website, under its own privacy policy and terms.
  • No scanning of your email, contacts, photos, or anything outside the app.

Where your data lives

Account and historical synced favorites data is stored with Supabase in the European Union (AWS eu-central-1, Frankfurt). Usage analytics is processed by PostHog's EU service. The new public catalog is delivered by Cloudflare; new favorites and the bag stay on your device. Purchases on Communite are handled under the retailer's privacy policy.

Your rights

You can delete your Finewild account in the app's Account tab. This removes your sign-in account and historical synced favorites and clears the account's cached favorites and addresses on that device. If account services are unavailable during the catalog transition, contact us below for help accessing or deleting your existing account. To request deletion of other data, including historical analytics associated with your account, contact us using the address below. You can remove device favorites by tapping their hearts and remove bag items from the bag.

You can request a copy of your data, a correction, or complete deletion of your account and everything tied to it at any time — email parisbrugemons@gmail.com and we will complete deletion within 30 days. If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have the rights granted by the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Links to the retailer

The Communite catalog update opens products on Communite. The retailer's privacy policy applies on its website. Product links may include a barcode to identify your chosen size; they do not contain your Finewild account, address or payment details. Earlier versions also linked to brands' own websites, whose policies apply there.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update this page and note the new effective date above.
