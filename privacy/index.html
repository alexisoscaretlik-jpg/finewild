Privacy Policy

Updated 7 September 2026

Finewild is a free discovery app for emerging fashion & design brands. You can browse without an account; signing in lets you save favorites. The app has no subscriptions, paid placement, or advertising. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

What we collect

  • Account. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your name and email address (with Sign in with Apple you can hide your real email). This is stored with our database provider, Supabase, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, eu-central-1).
  • Favorites. Your saved product identifiers are linked to your account and stored with Supabase so your favorites can sync between your devices. A local copy lets the app retain changes until a connection is available.
  • Usage analytics. We use PostHog's EU service to understand app use and improve reliability. It receives app lifecycle events and review-prompt events, app and device information (such as app version, device model, operating system, and language), and a randomly assigned identifier. When you sign in, analytics can be linked to your account identifier. PostHog can derive approximate location from your IP address; Finewild does not request your device's GPS location. This data is never sold or shared with brands in a form that identifies you.
  • Data kept on your device. Your bag and saved delivery addresses are stored locally. Finewild does not upload those addresses or collect card or payment information. You enter delivery and payment details on a brand's website when you choose to buy from that brand.

What we never do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers — the app contains no third-party ad SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of personal data, to anyone, ever.
  • No payment processing — buying from a brand happens on the brand's own website, under the brand's own privacy policy and terms.
  • No scanning of your email, contacts, photos, or anything outside the app.

Where your data lives

Account and synced favorites data is stored with Supabase in the European Union (AWS eu-central-1, Frankfurt). Usage analytics is processed by PostHog's EU service. Purchases from brands are handled by those brands under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can delete your Finewild account in the app's Account tab. This removes your sign-in account and synced favorites and clears the account's cached favorites and addresses on that device. To request deletion of other data, including historical analytics associated with your account, contact us using the address below.

You can request a copy of your data, a correction, or complete deletion of your account and everything tied to it at any time — email parisbrugemons@gmail.com and we will complete deletion within 30 days. If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have the rights granted by the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Links to brand websites

Finewild links out to independent brands' own websites. Once you leave the app for a brand's site, that brand's privacy policy applies. We do not add tracking or affiliate parameters to those links.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update this page and note the new effective date above.

Contact
