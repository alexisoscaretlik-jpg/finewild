Free access

Finewild is free to use. The current app offers no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paid membership. Browse the full catalog without an account; sign in to save favorites across devices.

Terms and privacy

Read the Terms of Use (Apple's standard EULA) and our Privacy Policy. Both links are also available in the app on the sign-in screen, before you sign in, and under Account → Settings.

Orders, shipping & returns

Finewild is a discovery app: when you buy, the purchase happens on the brand's own website with the brand's own checkout. For anything about an order — payment, shipping, returns, exchanges — please contact the brand you bought from directly. We never process payments and cannot see or modify orders.

Account

  • Sign-in issues. Finewild uses Sign in with Apple or Google. If sign-in fails, check that you're online and try again; if it persists, email us.
  • Delete your account. In the app, open Account → Delete account. If you cannot sign in, contact parisbrugemons@gmail.com for help. See our Privacy Policy for details.
