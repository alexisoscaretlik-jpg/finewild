Free access

Finewild is free to use. The current app offers no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paid membership. The Communite catalog update lets you browse, save favorites and keep a shopping list on your device without signing in.

Terms and privacy

Read the Terms of Use (Apple's standard EULA) and our Privacy Policy. Both links are also available in the app directly on Account, before you sign in, and under Account → Settings.

Orders, shipping & returns

In the Communite catalog update, purchases happen on Communite's website. Check current prices, sizes, stock, quantities and delivery destinations there before ordering. Your Finewild bag is a device shopping list; it does not reserve stock or transfer into the retailer's checkout. Contact Communite about its orders, shipping and returns. If you ordered on another brand's website through an earlier Finewild version, contact that seller. Finewild never processes payments and cannot see or modify orders.

Account

  • Sign-in issues. Account services may be unavailable during the catalog transition. The Communite catalog, device favorites and bag work without signing in. Email us if you need help accessing an existing account.
  • Delete your account. In the app, open Account → Delete account. If you cannot sign in, contact parisbrugemons@gmail.com for help. See our Privacy Policy for details.
